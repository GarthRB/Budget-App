Defend your fortress against endless waves. Tap & hold to fire at the horde. Every kill earns cash — survive the day, then spend it on deadlier weapons and tougher walls.

How to Play

🎯 Tap & hold anywhere to aim and fire your weapon at the attackers.

🧟 Enemies march toward your wall. If they reach it, they smash it down. Don't let your wall hit 0.

💰 Each kill drops cash. Clear every attacker to survive the day.

🛒 Between days, spend cash in the shop — upgrade damage, fire-rate, multishot, auto-turrets, and repair or reinforce your wall.

🔁 When your magazine empties it auto-reloads. Bigger mags & faster reloads keep you firing.
